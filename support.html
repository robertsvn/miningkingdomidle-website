Support

Email support@miningkingdomidle.com.

Include your phone model, operating system, game version and what happened. Please do not send passwords, login codes, payment card details or private save files.

Restore purchases

If a permanent purchase is missing, use Shop → Restore purchases with the same Apple or Google account you used to buy it.

If the benefit is still missing, email support with the product name and any error shown.

For payment or refund help, use Apple purchase support or Google Play support. Purchase and refund terms are explained in our Terms of use.

Cloud saves

Cloud backup is optional and works within the same platform. Android and Apple saves do not transfer between each other. Play on one device at a time and allow synchronization to finish.

A cloud copy can be behind your local progress. When asked to choose, review the available information carefully: selecting another save replaces your existing progress. Saves are not combined.

Rewarded videos

If a video will not load, check your connection and try again later.

If a reward is missing after a video finishes, email support with when it happened, which reward you chose and any message shown. Reward eligibility is explained in our Terms of use.
