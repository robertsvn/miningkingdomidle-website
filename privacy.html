Privacy policy

Updated 16 October 2026
Privacy contact: Developer, rsvngames — support@miningkingdomidle.com

Saved progress and cloud backup

The game stores your progress, settings and purchase records on your device so you can continue playing and retain purchased benefits.

Cloud backup is optional and off by default. If you enable it, your save is stored through Google Play Games on Android or GameKit/iCloud on Apple devices. There is no separate rsvngames player account or save database.

For help choosing a save or moving between devices, see Cloud saves in Support.

Purchases

Apple or Google processes payments. The game and Unity IAP use purchase receipts, transaction identifiers and purchase status to deliver purchases, record ownership and restore eligible permanent benefits.

We do not receive your full payment card details. The relevant store handles payment information under its own policies.

Ads and game services

Rewarded videos use Google Mobile Ads. Google’s User Messaging Platform handles applicable consent messages and advertising privacy choices.

These services can receive device information, advertising identifiers, IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, ad interactions and diagnostics to provide and measure advertising. The information processed depends on the service configuration, your privacy choices and platform permissions.

When Google makes privacy options available, you can review your choices through Ad privacy choices in the game’s Settings.

When Remove Ads is active, the game does not start new rewarded-ad requests. This does not delete information already held by providers or disable the other processing described in this policy.

Unity supplies the game engine and purchase integration. Its services can process installation, device, session and transaction information. Optional Unity Analytics collection and leaderboard score sharing are disabled in this version.

Website and support

GitHub Pages hosts this website and logs visitor IP addresses for security. The game also contacts this website to check connectivity before showing a rewarded video.

The website has no tracking scripts, registration form or automatic video embeds.

If you contact support, your email address, message and any attachments are processed through the email services used to receive and answer your request. We use this information to investigate the issue and respond.

Retention

Local saves remain until removed or replaced through the game or operating system. A gameplay reset keeps permanent purchase ownership and daily-use records.

Resetting progress, turning cloud backup off or uninstalling the game does not automatically delete cloud copies. The game can request their deletion through your platform, although another device or a pending upload may create a copy again.

Support correspondence is kept in the support mailbox to handle and follow up on requests and meet applicable legal record-keeping requirements.

Stores and other providers may retain records they control independently under their own policies and legal obligations.

Your choices and privacy requests

Contact support@miningkingdomidle.com with privacy questions or requests concerning your information, including support correspondence. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or withdrawal of consent.

We can handle requests about support correspondence directly. For information processed on our behalf, we will help handle applicable requests using the service provider’s tools. For records independently controlled by a provider, we may direct you to that provider’s request process. We can also explain the available game and platform controls for data on your device or in cloud saves.

Please describe your request clearly. We may need information to locate the relevant records and verify that the request concerns your information. We will respond within the time required by applicable law and explain any applicable limitations.

If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about a child’s personal information in connection with the game, contact us so we can review and address the concern.

You may also raise concerns with the relevant privacy regulator. In British Columbia, this includes the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for BC.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game’s services or information-handling practices change. The revised policy will appear on this page with an updated date.

Where required by law, we will provide additional notice and obtain consent before introducing new collection, use or disclosure of personal information.
