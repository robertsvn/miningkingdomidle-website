Privacy policy

Updated 16 October 2026 · rsvngames
Contact: support@miningkingdomidle.com

This page explains the data used by Mining Kingdom Idle and this website.

Saved progress

The game stores your progress, settings and purchase records on your device.

Cloud backup is off by default. If you turn it on, your save is stored through Google Play Games on Android or GameKit/iCloud on Apple devices. There is no separate publisher login. Android and Apple saves do not transfer between platforms.

Turning cloud backup off does not delete existing copies. The game can request deletion through your platform, though another device or a pending upload may create a copy again. Choosing another save replaces your existing progress. Saves are not combined.

Purchases

Apple or Google handles payment. The game and Unity IAP use receipts, transaction identifiers and purchase status to deliver purchases and restore permanent benefits. We do not receive full payment card details.

Ads and game services

Rewarded videos use Google Mobile Ads. Google’s User Messaging Platform handles applicable consent messages and privacy options. These services can receive device or advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostics.

Ad behavior depends on the service configuration, privacy choices and platform permissions. When Google makes privacy options available, use Ad privacy choices in the game’s Settings.

Unity supplies the game engine and purchase integration. Its services can process installation, device, session and transaction information. Optional Unity Analytics collection and leaderboard score sharing are disabled in this version.

Website and support

GitHub Pages hosts this website and logs visitor IP addresses for security. The game also contacts this site to check connectivity before rewarded videos. The site has no tracking scripts, sign-up form or automatic video embeds.

If you email support, your address, message and attachments are handled through the support mailbox to answer your request. Do not send passwords, payment card details or private save files.

Retention and requests

Local saves remain until removed or replaced through the game or operating system. A gameplay reset keeps permanent purchase ownership and daily-use records. It does not delete your cloud backup. Stores, cloud and SDK providers apply their own storage and retention policies.

Contact support@miningkingdomidle.com with privacy questions or requests about your information. Depending on your location, rights may include access, correction, deletion or objection. Requests for provider-held records may require that provider’s tools and identifying information.
