Privacy policy

Updated 16 October 2026
Privacy contact: Developer, rsvngames — support@miningkingdomidle.com

Saved progress and cloud backup

The game stores your progress, settings and purchase records on your device so you can continue playing and retain purchased benefits.

Cloud backup is optional and off by default. If you enable it, your save is stored through Google Play Games on Android or GameKit/iCloud on Apple devices. There is no separate rsvngames account. Android and Apple saves do not transfer between platforms.

Choosing another save replaces your existing progress. Saves are not combined, and a cloud copy may be behind your latest local progress.

Turning cloud backup off does not delete existing cloud copies. The game can request deletion through your platform, although another device or a pending upload may create a copy again.

Purchases

Apple or Google processes payments. The game and Unity IAP use purchase receipts, transaction identifiers and purchase status to deliver purchases, record ownership and restore eligible permanent benefits.

We do not receive your full payment card details. The relevant store handles payment information under its own policies.

Ads and game services

Rewarded videos use Google Mobile Ads. Google’s User Messaging Platform handles applicable consent messages and advertising privacy choices.

These services can receive device information, advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostics to provide and measure advertising. The information processed depends on the service configuration, your privacy choices and platform permissions.

When Google makes privacy options available, you can review your choices through Ad privacy choices in the game’s Settings.

When Remove Ads is active, the game does not start new rewarded-ad requests. This does not delete information already held by providers or disable the other processing described in this policy.

Unity supplies the game engine and purchase integration. Its services can process installation, device, session and transaction information. Optional Unity Analytics collection and leaderboard score sharing are disabled in this version.

Website and support

GitHub Pages hosts this website and logs visitor IP addresses for security. The game also contacts this website to check connectivity before showing a rewarded video.

The website has no tracking scripts, registration form or automatic video embeds.

If you contact support, your email address, message and any attachments are processed through the email services used to receive and answer your request. We use this information to investigate the issue and respond.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, payment card details or private save files.

Service providers and processing locations

The providers described above process information under their own privacy policies. Depending on the service, information may be processed outside your province or country and may be subject to the laws of that location.

Retention

Local saves remain until removed or replaced through the game or operating system. A gameplay reset keeps permanent purchase ownership and daily-use records. It does not delete your cloud backup.

We retain information under our control for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, including handling support requests, resolving related issues and meeting legal obligations. When that information is no longer needed, we delete it or remove identifying details, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Stores and other providers may retain records they control independently under their own policies and legal obligations.

Your choices and privacy requests

The cloud-backup and advertising controls described above let you manage those optional features.

You can contact support@miningkingdomidle.com to ask about information under our control, request access or correction, or raise a privacy concern. Depending on applicable law, you may also request deletion, withdraw consent or exercise other privacy rights.

Please describe your request clearly. We may ask for information reasonably needed to confirm your identity and locate the relevant records. We will respond within the time required by applicable law and explain any applicable limitations or exceptions.

Some requests concerning information independently controlled by a store or service provider may require that provider’s tools or request process.

If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about a child’s personal information in connection with the game, contact us so we can review and address the concern.

You may also raise concerns with the relevant privacy regulator. In British Columbia, this includes the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for BC.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game’s services or information-handling practices change. The revised policy will appear on this page with an updated date.

Where required by law, we will provide additional notice and obtain consent before introducing new collection, use or disclosure of personal information.
